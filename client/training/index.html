QUESTION WRITER'S GUIDE

how questions become platforms, and what fits on a projector Pack Studio →

In PLATFORMS, your answers aren't buttons — they're platforms in an arena, and the whole room physically jumps onto the one they believe. That has two consequences for writing: the question type picks the arena, and everything you write is painted on the big screen, so short text lands hard and long text shrinks. This page shows exactly what each type builds and how much room your words have. Diagrams below are generated from the real level code, so they can't go stale.

The standard deck (everything above the line) plays in both free-for-all and teams. Control cases only ever play when teams exist — write at least one case per team (three PGY teams → three cases), because the scheduler refuses to give one team a turn another team can't have. The Showdown is its own finale mode: no points, last one standing.
